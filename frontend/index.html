How LocalRaise Works
Simple, transparent, and built for real communities
01
Business Creates Campaign
A local business sets a funding goal, chooses a return model (revenue share or fixed return), and sets a repayment timeline. Takes 2 minutes.
02
Community Invests
Friends, family, and community members invest any amount of ETH. When the goal is reached, the business receives funds instantly on Base.
03
Everyone Earns
Business sends monthly payments back via the contract. Every investor earns proportional returns automatically. No banks. No delays.
