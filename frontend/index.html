❓ How LocalRaise Works

1
🏪
Business Lists Campaign
A local business creates a funding campaign with their goal, return model, and repayment timeline. Takes 2 minutes.
2
💰
Community Invests
Friends, family and community members invest any amount of ETH. When the goal is reached, the business receives funds instantly.
3
📈
Investors Earn Returns
Business sends monthly payments back to investors via the contract. Every investor earns proportional returns automatically.

📊 Revenue Share Model

Business shares a percentage of monthly profit with investors every month. Great for established businesses with steady revenue. Investors earn more when business does well.

📈 Fixed Return Model

Business pays back the principal amount plus a fixed percentage over the repayment period. Predictable returns for investors. Great for businesses with clear cash flow projections.
