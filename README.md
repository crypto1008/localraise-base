# LocalRaise 🚀

**Micro-investment platform for local businesses on Base Mainnet.**

Fund local businesses. Earn real returns. No banks needed.

## Contract Address
0xYOUR_CONTRACT_ADDRESS

## Basescan
https://basescan.org/address/0xYOUR_CONTRACT_ADDRESS

## The Problem
500 million small businesses globally need funding.
Banks reject 80% of them.
Moneylenders charge 40%+ interest.
Friends want to help but need returns.

## The Solution
LocalRaise lets communities micro-invest in local businesses.
Businesses get funded instantly.
Investors earn real returns onchain.
Everything is transparent and automatic.

## Return Models
- Revenue Share: Business shares % of monthly profit
- Fixed Return: Business repays principal + fixed interest

## Categories
- Food & Beverage
- Retail & Local Shop  
- Transport & Logistics

## How It Works
1. Business creates a funding campaign
2. Community members invest ETH
3. Goal reached → Business gets funded instantly
4. Business repays investors monthly
5. Platform earns 1.5% fee on successful raises
