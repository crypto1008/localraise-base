FOR BUSINESS OWNERS

How to fund your small business in India without a bank loan

May 19, 2025 6 min read By LocalRaise Team

If you own a small business in India — a chai stall, a kirana store, an auto-rickshaw fleet — you already know the feeling. You need ₹80,000 to buy new equipment or stock up before Diwali. You walk into the bank. You fill out fourteen forms. You wait three weeks. And then they say no.

This happens to 8 out of every 10 small business owners in India who apply for a bank loan. The banks want collateral you don't have, credit history you haven't built, and paperwork that takes months to gather.

The alternative — borrowing from a moneylender — sounds easier until you see the interest rate. 40%. Sometimes 80%. Sometimes more.

There is a third option that most business owners don't know about yet: community micro-investment using crypto on Base. Here's exactly how it works.

What is community micro-investment?

Community micro-investment means the people who know and trust your business — your friends, your family, your regular customers, your local community — pool small amounts of money to fund you. In return, they earn a share of your profits or a fixed return on what they put in.

Think of it like this: instead of asking one bank for ₹1,00,000, you ask 20 people for ₹5,000 each. Everyone wins. You get funded. They earn something back instead of just giving you a gift.

This concept is not new — the traditional Indian chit fund works on a similar principle. What is new is that blockchain technology makes this completely transparent, automatic, and trustworthy. Nobody can run away with the money. Every transaction is recorded permanently. Refunds happen automatically if a goal isn't reached.

How LocalRaise makes this possible

LocalRaise is a platform built on Base — a blockchain network created by Coinbase that charges roughly ₹1 per transaction in gas fees. Through LocalRaise, any small business owner can:

  1. Create a funding campaign in 5 minutes with no paperwork
  2. Set a goal (for example, 0.1 ETH — about ₹25,000)
  3. Choose a return model — either sharing a % of your monthly profit or paying a fixed return
  4. Share the campaign link with your community
  5. Receive funds automatically once the goal is reached
Real example: Ramesh owns a chai shop in Dehradun. He needs ₹60,000 for new equipment. He creates a campaign on LocalRaise with a goal of 0.25 ETH, offering 20% of monthly profits for 12 months. 8 people from his community each invest around 0.03 ETH. Goal reached in 4 days. Ramesh gets funded. His investors earn returns every month directly to their wallets.

Step-by-step: How to create your first campaign

1
Set up MetaMask wallet on Base
Download MetaMask (browser extension or mobile app). Add Base Mainnet network. You'll need a tiny amount of ETH on Base for gas fees — about ₹50 worth is more than enough for all transactions.
2
Go to LocalRaise and click "List Business"
Fill in your business name, description, location, and category. Be specific about what you need the money for — investors are more likely to fund campaigns with clear, honest descriptions.
3
Choose your return model and set your goal
Revenue share: you share X% of monthly profit for Y months. Fixed return: you pay back the principal plus an agreed percentage. Revenue share works better if your monthly income varies. Fixed return works better if you have predictable cash flow.
4
Set your funding deadline
Between 7 and 90 days. We recommend 14–30 days for most campaigns. This creates urgency for your community to invest. If the goal isn't reached by the deadline, everyone is automatically refunded — no manual work needed.
5
Share your campaign link everywhere
WhatsApp groups, Instagram stories, local Facebook groups. Tell people exactly what you need the money for and what they'll earn back. The clearer your story, the faster you'll get funded.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know crypto to use LocalRaise?

You need a basic MetaMask wallet and a small amount of ETH on Base (about ₹50 worth for gas fees). The platform itself is straightforward — if you can use a smartphone app, you can use LocalRaise.

What if I can't repay investors?

LocalRaise has a default protection system. If you miss payments for 60 days, your account is flagged and you won't be able to create new campaigns. Think of it like your reputation — keep repaying and your reputation score grows, making it easier to raise funds again in the future.

How much ETH do I need to get started?

You only need around 0.0005 ETH (about ₹10–15) to create a campaign — just enough for the gas fee. The campaign itself doesn't cost anything except that gas.

Can my investors be people who don't use crypto?

Currently LocalRaise requires investors to have a MetaMask wallet. For now, this means your investors need basic crypto knowledge. As adoption grows, this barrier will decrease.

The bottom line

If you've been rejected by a bank or you're tired of paying 40% interest to a moneylender, community micro-investment on LocalRaise is worth trying. You set the terms. Your community gets to earn returns on money they'd otherwise just give you as a gift. And everything is tracked automatically on the blockchain — no disputes, no missing payments, no confusion.

The first campaign is the hardest part. Once your community sees you repay your first round of investors, the next campaign will be faster and bigger.
