How LocalRaise works
1
Business lists a campaign
Any small business sets a funding goal with a return offer and deadline. No bank paperwork. No collateral needed.
2
Community invests
Anyone invests any amount. Goal reached → business gets funds. Goal not met → everyone is automatically refunded.
3
Earn real returns
Business sends monthly payments through the smart contract. Every investor gets proportional returns automatically.
