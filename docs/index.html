How LocalRaise works
1
Business lists a campaign
A chai shop, kirana store, or auto-rickshaw owner creates a funding goal with a return offer and deadline. Takes 2 minutes.
2
Community invests
Anyone can invest any amount. When the goal is reached the business receives funds instantly. If the goal isn't met, everyone is automatically refunded.
3
Earn real returns
Business sends monthly payments back through the contract. Every investor earns proportional returns automatically — no middlemen, no trust required.
